Vision and Approach

Vision

To be the leading academic-based consultancy service provider in delivering high-impact services in consultancy, commercialization and technology licensing that improve clients' competitiveness.

Objectives

  • To provide high impact knowledge transfer services that contributes towards innovation and technological upgrading of Hong Kong and the Pearl River Delta region.
  • To be the preferred partner for clients seeking consultancy services and establish long-term partnership for repeated services.
  • To continually keep industry and business community abreast of new technologies and innovative product ideas from the University for increasing our client's market competitiveness.

Our Approach

Customer-centric Approach
At PTeC, we provide one-stop services to address the specific needs of our clients. Whether they are a private company, multinational corporation, SME, government department or NGO, we can help them establish relationships with either an individual or a multi-disciplinary team of experts.

Professional Consultants and Resources
With a wide range of skills and expertise available from the 1,200 plus top-notch professors from eight Faculties and Schools, we can pool a team of multi-disciplinary consultants with the experience to deliver scalable and flexible professional services of any nature to suit clients' requirements. A wide array of PolyU's state-of-the art facilities and equipment in over 100 research centres and laboratories are made available for conducting applied R & D and laboratory testing as required.

Total Quality Commitment
We are the first university consultancy operation in Hong Kong accredited with ISO9001:2008 quality management system. Our professional consultants with extensive project management experience and strict quality control measures ensure prompt and effective results to clients' total satisfaction.
