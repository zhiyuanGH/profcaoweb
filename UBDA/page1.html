UBDA Aims and Objectives

The University has announced the establishment of the University Research Facility in
Big Data Analytics (UBDA) in Oct. 2016.

What Bigdata?

Big data is characterized by its volume, velocity and variety, and applies to information that exceeds the processing capacity of conventional database and computing systems.

 Such big data sets are generated from different sources including science, business, medicine and healthcare, social media and education. The increase in large volume, great variety and real-time data allows for improved measurement and monitoring, and consequently seeing things with larger scope, more details and greater clarity, in a way we couldn’t before. 

Why UBDA?

Big data provides us unprecedented opportunities to extract patterns and generate insights for optimizing decision making and predictions. However, it also imposes great challenges.



The need to provide a platform to develop, support, service and sustain research into big data analytics is becoming increasingly urgent for PolyU. Lowering the barrier to entry into this cross discipline research domain is a key ingredient to encourage data centric research practices at PolyU.


How UBDA?

The vision of UBDA is to develop PolyU’s research and knowledge competencies in a
direction which contributes to a sustainable development of big data technologies that is responsive and dynamically adaptable to diverse requirements and applications.


The team led by Prof. Jiannong Cao of COMP and Prof. Xiaojun Chen of AMA has extensive experience in diverse research in related areas and is well equipped to take up the challenges and play the coordination role.
